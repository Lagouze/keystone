# C2 — Permissionless default settlement

`settle_default() -> VaultState` implements interfaces section 3 without changing
its signatures, types or error codes. It accepts no recipient or amount from
the caller. It requires MarginCall, ledger timestamp >= call_deadline and a
positive shortfall, then transfers exactly the stored locked amount to the
configured bank. It does not require authorization from any vault role.

After a successful transfer it sets Defaulted, clears locked, required,
shortfall and call_deadline, preserves available and epoch, refreshes instance
TTL and emits `defaulted` with named data `{amount: i128}`. A rejected SAC
transfer leaves the margin call and collateral unchanged. Terminal withdrawal
never rebalances; deposits and margin publications return Defaulted.

### Real settlement fixture

Vault: `CALNED2G3Y2XLDMGZMFR2KR7RIWSL3FN36QAL4TNC27233AC5BJNLV4H`.
This fixture uses existing local test roles and their fictitious **KSC1** SAC,
not the project's KMMF/KBOND asset or accounts. The local fund had 700 KSC1
before this run and 690 afterwards; the bank went from 0 to 10 and the vault
from 0 to 10 to 0. The vault keeper has no KSC1 trustline (verified on Horizon),
so the SAC balance query for that account reports a missing trustline rather
than a numeric balance; it received no tokens. The successful transfer event
names only the configured bank as recipient.

The margin call locked 100000000 base units with a 50000000 shortfall and
deadline 1792970847. A premature simulation returned GracePeriodRunning.
Settlement confirmed at ledger timestamp 1792970867, after the real 90-second
grace period. Its fee payer was a fourth account distinct from fund, bank and
keeper, with **zero external Soroban auth entries**. Its envelope signature,
SAC transfer and single `defaulted {amount: 100000000}` event were decoded and
verified. Final status is Defaulted, epoch remains 1 and the four cleared
fields are zero. Read-only simulations subsequently returned Defaulted for
deposit/margin, NotInMarginCall for replay and InsufficientAvailable for withdrawal.
These rejected simulations are not transactions and have no transaction hash.
